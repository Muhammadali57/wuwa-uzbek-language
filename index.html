THE REQUEST

One language.
One more voice.

Wuthering Waves connects players across cultures, countries and languages. We believe Uzbek-speaking players should have the opportunity to experience the world of Solaris-3 in their own language.

This project is a community effort to show Kuro Games that there is real, measurable interest in Uzbek language support.

OUR MESSAGE TO KURO GAMES

A respectful request
from the community.

Dear Kuro Games,

Wuthering Waves has brought together players from many different cultures and languages around the world.

We respectfully ask Kuro Games to consider adding Uzbek language support to Wuthering Waves. This campaign represents players who would love to experience Solaris-3 in Uzbek, alongside supporters from around the world who want to help make this possible.

Thank you for listening to your community.

— Wuthering Waves community
07 /

FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Why are you requesting Uzbek language support?

Because Uzbek-speaking players want a more accessible way to experience Wuthering Waves and the wider community wants to show that demand clearly.

Does voting cost anything?

No. The campaign is intended to be free to join.

Can players outside Uzbekistan vote?

Yes. The campaign is global and supporters from any country can participate.

Is this official?

No. This is a community-led campaign and is not affiliated with Kuro Games.

Will my email be public?

No. Email addresses should remain private and will not be shown on the public supporter wall.
